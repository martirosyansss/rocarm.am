Quality Control
At ROCARM, we are passionate about providing our customers with more than just delicious and refreshing beverages. We want you to enjoy our products with complete confidence, knowing they are produced with the highest standards of safety and quality. That's why we are proud to be certified in both ISO 22000 (Food Safety Management) and ISO 9001 (Quality Management).
ISO 22000 Certified
Hazard control: We proactively identify and address potential risks throughout the production process.
Full traceability: Every step of a product's journey is tracked for efficient issue handling.
Continuous improvement: We constantly update our food safety practices with the latest advancements.
ISO 9001 Certified
Customer Focus: We prioritize exceeding your expectations by understanding your needs.
Continuous Improvement: We constantly refine our processes to ensure consistent quality in every product.
Efficient Operations: We manage resources effectively for optimal production and delivery.
EAC Certified
EAC Certified: We meet the high quality and safety standards of the Eurasian Economic Union.
Trusted Quality Across the EAEU: Our EAC certification guarantees adherence to strict safety and quality regulations.
The Untamed Spirit of the Armenian Mountains
Garni Crystalline's journey begins in the heart of the Armenian wilderness, where pristine mountain springs bubble forth from ancient rocks. We capture this natural wonder at its source, preserving its purity and refreshing taste. Through a gentle filtration process, we remove any impurities while ensuring the essential minerals remain, delivering the true essence of mountain refreshment in every bottle.

Crafted with European Precision
The ROCARM workshop is equipped with state-of-the-art machinery from leading European companies, ensuring the highest quality standards in our production process. This commitment to excellence is evident in every bottle of Garni Crystalline we produce.
​
Key European Equipment​
Refrigeration: Maintaining optimal temperatures with a Climaveneta refrigerator (Italy).
Compression: Ensuring efficient operation with a Kaeser compressor (Germany).
Bottling Line: Streamlining the bottling process with a high-performance line from STM (Poland).
Packaging System: Delivering a polished and secure final product with a Siat packaging system (Italy).
